# Release notes

## 1.0.0 (versionCode 1)

First release.

- Add, edit, pause and cancel subscriptions with weekly, monthly, quarterly, yearly or custom cycles
- Monthly and yearly totals in your home currency, with correct month-end and leap-year billing dates
- Home dashboard: next charge, spending trend, potential savings and a smart insight
- Payment calendar
- Insights: yearly total, largest subscription, category concentration, low usage, price increases
- Optional reminders before a charge, a monthly summary and a savings note — all off by default
- Export to JSON or CSV; delete everything with one tap
- Dark and light themes; no account, no analytics, no internet permission

### Play Console "What's new" (500 characters)
Know what you pay, before it charges you. Track every subscription, see monthly and yearly totals,
get reminded before charges, and find what you could save — all private, all on your device.
