# SUBZERO Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-09-13

SUBZERO is a subscription tracker that runs entirely on your device.

**What we collect:** nothing. SUBZERO has no user accounts and uses no analytics, advertising or
crash-reporting services.

**Enhanced assistant answers (optional, off by default):** if you turn this on in
Settings → Assistant, then each time you ask the assistant a question, SUBZERO sends that
question and a summary of your active subscriptions (service name, amount, billing cycle,
category, how often you said you use it, and the next payment date) over HTTPS to the AI
provider configured in the app, which generates the answer. Your notes, your name and your
payment history are never sent. Turning the switch off stops all network use immediately;
with it off the assistant answers from your device alone.

**What stays on your device:** the subscriptions you enter (names, prices, billing dates,
categories, notes and how often you say you use them) and your preferences (currency, theme,
notification choices, optional display name). This data is stored in the app's private storage
and may be included in your Android backup, which Google encrypts.

**Notifications:** reminders are off by default. If you turn them on, SUBZERO asks for the
notification permission and schedules reminders locally. No data is sent anywhere.

**Your control:** you can export all your data as JSON or CSV, and delete everything, from
Settings → Data & storage. Uninstalling the app removes all its data.

**Children:** SUBZERO is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

**Languages:** SUBZERO is available in English and Arabic and follows your phone's language;
on Android 13 and later you can pick a different language for this app alone.

**Changes:** if a future version changes what is sent or adds another online feature, this
policy and the Play Store data safety section will be updated before that version is released.
