## Full description (4000)
SUBZERO makes your recurring spending visible.

See everything
Add the subscriptions you pay for — streaming, AI tools, cloud storage, software, fitness — and
SUBZERO turns them into one clear number: what you pay per month, and per year.

Predict your spending
A payment calendar and a next-charge card show what is coming before it hits. Turn on an
optional reminder and SUBZERO tells you the day before a subscription charges you.

Find what you are wasting
Tell SUBZERO how often you actually use each service. It spots the ones you rarely use, the ones
that raised their price, and the categories where you pay for several similar things — and shows
what you could save. It never tells you what to cancel; it shows you the numbers.

Private by design
Everything stays on your phone. No account, no sign-up, no analytics, and the app does not even
ask for internet access. Export your data any time as JSON or CSV, or delete it with one tap.

Built for clarity
A calm, dark interface designed around your numbers. Fast, offline, and respectful of your
attention: nothing is on by default.
